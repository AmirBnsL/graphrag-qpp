# Quantized Cross-Family 2026 Model Pairing (Gemma 4 and Qwen 3.5/3.8) for Context-Safe VRAM

We use `Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` (with an in-memory text-embedding cache across sessions and ablation stages) for dense embeddings, and pair quantized 8B–12B models from Alibaba's **Qwen 3.5** family (`RedHatAI/Qwen3.5-9B-FP8-dynamic` with `<think>` reasoning disabled for concise RAG answers) and Google's **Gemma 4** family (`google/gemma-4-12B-it-qat-w4a16-ct` in `compressed-tensors` W4A16 format) as the RAG Generator and Cross-Family LLM Judge via `vLLM`, loading only one LLM into VRAM at a time.

On a 24 GB Colab Pro L4 GPU, unquantized 9B–12B models consume 18–24 GB for weights alone, starving the `vLLM` KV cache when processing 5-passage RAG contexts across batched prompts. Running `RedHatAI/Qwen3.5-9B-FP8-dynamic` (~9 GB weights) and `google/gemma-4-12B-it-qat-w4a16-ct` (~6.5 GB weights) sequentially with explicit `llm_engine.shutdown()` teardown reserves 11–14 GB of VRAM exclusively for prompt context and KV cache while preserving 2026 9B–12B reasoning quality and eliminating self-preference bias.
